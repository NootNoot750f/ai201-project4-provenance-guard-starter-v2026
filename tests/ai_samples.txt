## A06 — Technical (topic: how a computer joins a Wi-Fi network)
Connecting a computer to a Wi-Fi network involves several coordinated steps. The device first detects available networks by listening for signals broadcast by nearby access points. After a password is entered, the devices complete an authentication process that verifies the credentials and establishes encryption. Finally, the router assigns the computer an IP address, typically through DHCP, which allows it to communicate with other devices and the wider internet. Each stage supports secure and reliable access.

## A09 — Short opinion (topic: reliability versus speed in computers)

Over the long run, dependable performance tends to matter far more than marginal gains in processing power.

## A10 — Longer reflection (topic: building a daily writing habit)

One common obstacle is perfectionism, where the urge to polish each sentence interrupts the flow of drafting. Separating the process into distinct stages, drafting first and editing later, can make the work more manageable. Over time, giving oneself permission to write imperfectly often leads to steadier progress and a more sustainable routine.
